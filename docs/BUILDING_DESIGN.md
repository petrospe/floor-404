## Intended scope
15 above-ground floors (1–15) plus basements B1 and B2: 17 principal levels. A ground-floor exit sequence may be separate if needed; resolve numbering in existing scenes before implementation.

## Spatial principles
- Vary room size, shape, density, and corridor topology between floors.
- Avoid rooms that are almost all single-door dead ends. Use loops, hubs, through-rooms, and multi-entry clusters where appropriate.
- The proposed 25% one-door / 40% two-door / 20% three-door / 15% four-plus-door distribution is an exploratory design guideline, **not** a rigid generation rule.
- Every mandatory objective must remain reachable. Validate navigation/connectivity after changes.
- Furnish rooms according to function; use existing assets and measured performance budgets rather than indiscriminate prop spam.
- Preserve completed Floor 3 and Floor 4 geometry unless a narrowly scoped fix is approved.
