# Floor 404 — Game Vision

## Core concept
A software engineer on the fourth floor of a large multinational technology company leaves his office at the end of his shift and finds everyone gone. He explores a vast, apparently ordinary corporate building while its architecture, systems, and records increasingly undermine his confidence in reality.

**Tagline:** Reality Not Found.

## Experience pillars
1. Psychological and liminal unease rather than conventional horror.
2. Exploration of a coherent but unsettling office building.
3. Environmental storytelling through objects, terminals, notes, and spaces.
4. Varied puzzles integrated into believable building infrastructure.
5. Philosophical uncertainty: can a conscious observer verify that its world is real?

## Constraints
- No monsters, combat, chase sequences, or jump scares.
- Do not rely on constant music: use office ambience, mechanical sounds, sparse suspense music, and silence.
- Avoid overexplaining the mystery or declaring a canonical answer in player-facing content.
- Avoid excessive glowing markers, gamey collectibles, or repetitive puzzle gates.
- Respect accessibility: readable notes, optional transcripts, and legible interaction cues.

## Tone progression
Normality → absence → anomalies → doubt → possible explanations → unresolved ending.
